Refund & Cancellation Policy

Fair, transparent cancellation terms — because plans change, and that's okay. Last updated: June 2026.

1. Free cancellation window

You may cancel your booking free of charge up to 7 days before your trip start date. We will refund 100% of the amount paid, minus any payment gateway fees.

2. Cancellation schedule — tour packages

When you cancel Refund
7+ days before trip start 100% refund
4–6 days before trip start 50% refund
1–3 days before trip start No refund
No-show / same day No refund

3. Cancellation schedule — individual experiences

When you cancel Refund
48+ hours before the experience 100% refund
24–48 hours before 50% refund
Less than 24 hours / no-show No refund

4. Cancellations by Zaffron Valley

If we cancel your trip or experience for any reason — including safety concerns, severe weather, or host unavailability — you will receive a full 100% refund or the option to reschedule at no extra cost.

5. How to cancel

  • Email us at zaffronvalleytours@gmail.com with your booking reference and reason for cancellation.
  • Or message us on WhatsApp at +91 60065 75829.
  • We will confirm your cancellation and refund amount within 24 hours.

6. Refund processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. Bank processing times may add 2–5 additional working days. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is initiated.

7. Modifications

Want to change your dates or itinerary instead of cancelling? We're happy to help. Modifications made 7+ days before the trip start date are free of charge, subject to availability. Changes within 7 days may incur a rebooking fee depending on what's involved.

8. Force majeure

In the event of extraordinary circumstances beyond anyone's control — natural disasters, government-imposed travel restrictions, pandemics, civil unrest — we will work with you to reschedule your trip or provide a full refund. No cancellation penalty applies in force majeure situations.

9. Contact

Questions about cancellations or refunds? Reach us at zaffronvalleytours@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 60065 75829.
