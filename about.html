Built in the valley.
Run by Kashmiris.

We’re not a booking aggregator in a faraway office. We’re locals who grew tired of watching guests get overcharged and our own people get squeezed, so we built the company we’d want our friends to use.

Paradise deserved a fair deal

Search “Kashmir trip” and you’ll find a hundred near-identical sites, and a hundred reviews warning about the same things: surprise charges, black-market gondola tickets, guides on commission, and money vanishing to middlemen who never set foot in the valley.

Zaffron Valley removes all of it. One fixed, itemised price. Every host ID-verified. A real local team reachable 24/7. And the large majority of what you pay stays with the Kashmiri families who actually host you. That’s the whole company.

Four promises we put in writing

Kashmiriyat (the valley’s old ethic of hospitality and fairness) isn’t decoration for us. It’s the rulebook.

Transparent always

Every price is itemised before you pay a rupee. No “local taxes,” no day-of extras, no commission games.

Verified, not random

Hosts, guides, drivers and houseboats are ID-checked, reviewed and insured before they ever appear on the site.

Locally owned

100% Kashmiri-owned and operated. 80%+ of what you spend stays with the people who host you.

Leave it better

Small groups, off-peak routing away from crowds, and a share of every booking funds Dal Lake and trail clean-ups.

Curated trips on top, an open marketplace beneath

Two ways to travel, one honest price.

Curated packages

For travellers who want it handled. Local designers build a transparent, day-by-day itinerary you can adjust freely, then we run every detail on the ground.

The experiences marketplace

For travellers who want to build their own. Browse verified hosts (cooks, weavers, boatmen, shepherds, musicians) and add them à la carte. The differentiator no competitor offers.
